## Plan

Base: P35 (`KAIRO_HAIR=proj`, the committed package `samples/SpineAvatarLab/Resources/Raw/Avatars/kairo.spineavatar`, sha256 ed0f8713…; a rebuild from the generator at master reproduces its GLB byte for byte, sha256 5987904c…). The owner's reference bundle (`kairo-hair-claude-references-final.zip`) carries six diagnostic renders of another package: the one in the app bundle this morning was the 2026-10-11 strand-layer package from commit 08b3c49 (hash-identical), restored by the previous session; the owner confirmed P35 is the version to work from.

Baseline P35 (tools in `~/.cache/spine-avatar-lab/kairo/tools`): one hair component (35 077 faces), no loose pieces; `hairview.py` gaps 73–734 px and head-through-hair 15–792 px per 500 px tile over poses 0,0 / 0,-35 / 70,35 / -70,-35 x views 0/±60/±90/180/top; hair texture 4608x2304 RGBA PNG 6.4 MB with 19.7 % of the texels cut (alpha < 128); eyes 102 px, cheek flakes ~22 800 px (changelog #524).

Defects to fix, in the owner's order:
1. The bob's side in front of the ear reads ragged from three quarters. Diagnosis from the P35 hair-on-grey grid: the side views cut the envelope strand by strand where they show cheek, on a surface that lies further forward than the hair in those views. The geometric cause is the envelope's closing (8 voxels, 2 cm), which bridges the concavity between the bob's side and the cheek in front of the ear; the alpha cut then has a slab to remove. Fix at the source: `hairenv.py` carves the closed volume with the side and back views' hair silhouettes (a visual hull through Pixal3D's cameras, each view limited to its own half of the head, the masks dilated for a tolerance), so the envelope ends where the views' hair ends and the alpha only trims strand tips. Then regularise the baked alpha: alpha-1 islands and alpha-0 pinholes smaller than a few texels go.
2. A hidden root layer (`roots` material, its own primitive): the skull's skin faces under the hair (hair weight > 0.5 from `compose()`'s coverage field: the hair volume's ray test, the photo's closed hair mask, the scalp behind the face and the ears) lifted 0.5–3 mm along smoothed normals, never under the face grid, never on the auricles; textured dark indigo with the fine root strands (`hair_style` at a low base luminance), shaded by the baked occlusion, baked into box charts at 512 px tiles with an alpha that is the coverage feathered and the views' skin cut (so its front edge never descends onto the forehead or the ears). MASK, emissive like the hair. Through any gap or cut in the hair layer the eye meets roots, in the lab and in the owner's hair-on-grey review mode alike.
3. The bangs' lock's thin end at the parting, only if the first two leave time: not touched otherwise (the face-mask drop rule stays on).
4. Size: measured before and after (package, GLB, triangles, textures).

Verification: the lab (native renderer) grid poses 0,0 / 0,±35 / 70,35 / -70,-35 x views 0/±60/±90/180 for the real package and the hair-on-grey variant, 1200 pt close-ups at 0/±45/±60/±90/180, `hairparts.py`, `hairview.py`, `eyecheck.py`, `cheekcheck.py`, `seethrough.py`; the full 13 x 6 matrix on the candidate; the phone and ~/Applications at the end.

## Decisions

- Why the tapered-tips track stops here: the hair is one envelope cut by the views' masks, and the envelope does not follow the views' strands in depth, so every strand-level cut on it reads as slivers from the other angles; the dead ends measured on the way (inner-sheet/lip classification: the winding is mixed and "faces down" is the outer sheet's own curl; per-pixel "behind the outermost": 59-82 % of the hair at the silhouette; radial thinning: dissolved in the isosurface's blur) are the same limit. The reference's tips need hair as cards per lock (strips extracted from the views' strands with their own tapered alpha and flow), a different reconstruction rather than another rule on this one.

- The owner decided mid-round (lab screenshots): the lump past the photo's outline beside her right ear is a defect to fix (the front carve), the nape's pinholes too (the view gap rule, the eroded in-head volume, the hole fill), and the face grid's skirt may change: it renders roots now, so the skin-to-hair transition reads as roots rather than skin triangles.

- The reference bundle's six diagnostic renders were of the 2026-10-11 strand package (commit 08b3c49), not of P35; the owner confirmed P35 as the base, so the task's "current output" is P35 and the prompt's defect list is read against P35's own open points (#524 handoff).
- The root cap takes its coverage from `compose()`'s hair weight alone. A first version also applied the projector's view cut and lost three quarters of its texels: the views' depth buffers hold the envelope only, so the back of the cap counted as seen through the face from the front, and the hug rule cut it wherever the photo shows skin.
- The front carve uses the photo through `project()` (the crop camera the whole generator works in), not Pixal3D's front camera with the matte: the matte's feathered edge kept the bulges, and the carve must know the head's depth to spare the hair behind the head on the same rays.
