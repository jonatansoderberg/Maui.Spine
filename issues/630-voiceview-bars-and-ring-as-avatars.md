# Issue #630 — VoiceView: bars and ring as avatars

**GitHub:** https://github.com/jonatansoderberg/Maui.Spine/issues/630
**Branch:** issue/630-voiceview-bars-and-ring-as-avatars
**Status:** In Progress
**Plan:** [docs/plans/spine-avatar.md](../docs/plans/spine-avatar.md)

## Plan

VoiceView's `bars` and `ring` become ambient `.spineavatar` packages drawn through the avatar path, replacing their SkiaSharp `VoiceVisual` classes. The owner chose to replace them fully (not a second route beside them) and to move bars and ring only; `orb` and the simple face `avatar` stay as they are.

- **Format:** spine2d parameters per band, `inputBand0`–`inputBand23` and `outputBand0`–`outputBand23`, beside `inputLevel`, `outputLow`…, so a vector avatar can draw a spectrum. Vector rather than SkSL: a shader renders through a 112 px buffer on CPU canvases (Android, Windows), which blurs bars.
- **Colours:** VoiceView's `InputColor` and `OutputColor` reach the avatar as theme-slot overrides (`input`, `output`).
- **Packages:** `bars.spineavatar` and `ring.spineavatar` (ambient, spine2d), generated by a script in the repository and packed with the spine-avatar tool; in `samples/Avatars` and embedded in the library.
- **One package:** `Plugin.Maui.Spine.Controls.Avatar.Voice` depends on `Plugin.Maui.Spine.Controls.Voice`, so the voice view can host avatars itself only if the two become one: `VoiceAvatarDriver`, `VoiceAvatar` and the `character` kind move into Controls.Voice, which references Controls.Avatar. Neither package has shipped (v0.1.9 predates both).
- **Showcase:** the Voice visuals page draws bars and ring as avatars.

## Changes

- spine2d parameters per band: `inputBand0`–`inputBand23` and `outputBand0`–`outputBand23` (`Spine2dVocabulary`, the validator, `bindings.schema.json`, `Spine2dRenderer.ApplyBands`), documented in the format's parameter table.
- `AvatarView.SetThemeColor(slot, color)`: a theme slot's colour from the app, in light and dark; `AvatarSurface.ThemeColors`; the spine2d and SkSL renderers honour it (glTF has no slots).
- `bars.spineavatar` and `ring.spineavatar` (ambient, spine2d 1.1 with strokes, about 50 and 100 nodes) from `samples/SpineAvatarLab/Tools/voice_visuals.py`, packed with `spine-avatar pack --strip` into `samples/Avatars`: one path node per band scaled from its own end, `input`/`output` slots, duplex (both sides react in Listening, Thinking, Speaking and Interrupted), the microphone's side at 30 % when muted, and under Reduce Motion a still bell of bars (bars) or two rings (ring) on the levels instead of the spectrum.
- `Plugin.Maui.Spine.Controls.Avatar.Voice` merged into `Plugin.Maui.Spine.Controls.Voice`: `VoiceAvatarDriver` (in `Core/`, still built for net10.0 and tested there), `VoiceAvatar`, and `CharacterVoiceVisual` generalised to `AvatarVoiceVisual`, which hosts the `character` and, with a built-in package, `bars` and `ring`, coloured from the view's `InputColor`/`OutputColor`. Controls.Voice references Controls.Avatar and embeds the two packages; `UseSpineVoiceView()` calls `UseSpineAvatar()` and registers the three avatar kinds. `BarsVisual` and `RingVisual` are gone; the solution, the package filter and the Showcase drop the old project.
- Tests: `VoiceVisualAvatarTests` (each bar and tick follows its own band, the host's colours replace the slots, Reduce Motion shows the level and not the spectrum; renders in `bin/…/sheets/` for review), `VoiceDriverTests.BarsAndRingGetTheSpectrumWhileTheConversationIsLive`; the Skia bars and ring tests in Controls.Voice.Tests removed with the classes.
- Showcase: the Voice visuals page says which visuals are avatars. Checked on the iOS simulator: bars and ring move with the reference speech and the microphone.
- Docs: voice, avatars and packages wiki pages, the Controls.Voice README, the spine-controls and spine-avatar skills, the plan (C2b, package map).

## Decisions

- Replace fully, bars and ring only (the owner, 2026-10-19): orb and the simple face stay canvas visuals; `AddVisual`/`AddVisualView` stay for visuals of one's own.
- **Vector, not SkSL.** A shader is drawn on the CPU into a buffer of at most 112 px and scaled up (Android, Windows), which blurs bars; spine2d paths stay sharp everywhere. On Metal both would have been sharp.
- **The packages merge.** Avatar.Voice depended on Controls.Voice, so Controls.Voice could only host avatars by taking the avatar code in; neither package had shipped (v0.1.9 predates both), so nothing breaks for users.
- **One path node per band**, rotated to point up, down or outward and scaled along it from its inner end: a group plus a shape per band gave the ring 198 nodes, over the format's 128.
- **An empty first root node** in both packages: the scheduler's springs (squash, tilt, lift) move the first root, and a spectrum that wobbles reads as wrong.
- **Avatars keep their aspect** (`fit: contain`), unlike the Skia bars, which filled any rectangle: in a very wide, short VoiceView the bars are smaller than before.
- Posters are rendered under Reduce Motion by the tool, so bars' poster is its bell of bars.
