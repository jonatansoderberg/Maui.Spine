# Issue #630 — VoiceView: bars and ring as avatars

**GitHub:** https://github.com/jonatansoderberg/Maui.Spine/issues/630
**Branch:** issue/630-voiceview-bars-and-ring-as-avatars
**Status:** In Progress
**Plan:** [docs/plans/spine-avatar.md](../docs/plans/spine-avatar.md) (C2b)

## Plan

Started as "VoiceView's bars and ring become avatars". After the first round the owner asked why there were several things for the same job ("min tanke med avatar var att visualisera ljud input/output") and decided: **AvatarView is the one visual for a voice.** VoiceView, its orb, its canvas face and the `VoiceVisual`/`VoiceVisualView` contracts go; bars and ring stay as built-in avatars; the Showcase draws every voice page through AvatarView.

- **Format:** spine2d parameters per band, `inputBand0`–`inputBand23` and `outputBand0`–`outputBand23`, so a vector avatar draws a spectrum. Vector rather than SkSL: a shader renders through a 112 px buffer on CPU canvases (Android, Windows), which blurs bars.
- **Built in:** `bars.spineavatar` and `ring.spineavatar`, generated by a script in the repository, packed with the spine-avatar tool into `samples/Avatars` and embedded in Controls.Avatar; `AvatarSource.BuiltIn(name)`, and in XAML `Source="bars"`.
- **Packages:** `Plugin.Maui.Spine.Controls.Voice` (and the briefly merged `Controls.Avatar.Voice`) removed; `VoiceAvatarDriver` moves into Controls.Avatar, which references Spine.Voice. Neither package had shipped (v0.1.9 predates both).

## Changes

- spine2d parameters per band: `inputBand0`–`inputBand23` and `outputBand0`–`outputBand23` (`Spine2dVocabulary`, the validator, `bindings.schema.json`, `Spine2dRenderer.ApplyBands`), documented in the format's parameter table.
- `AvatarView.SetThemeColor(slot, color)`: a theme slot's colour from the app, in light and dark; `AvatarSurface.ThemeColors`; the spine2d and SkSL renderers honour it (glTF has no slots).
- `bars.spineavatar` and `ring.spineavatar` (ambient, spine2d 1.1 with strokes, about 50 and 100 nodes) from `samples/SpineAvatarLab/Tools/voice_visuals.py`: one path node per band scaled from its own end; the answer in the `output` slot (warm), the microphone in the `accent` slot (the app's accent, `AccentColor`); duplex (both sides react in Listening, Thinking, Speaking and Interrupted); the microphone's side at 30 % when muted; under Reduce Motion a still bell of bars (bars) or two rings (ring) on the levels instead of the spectrum.
- `AvatarSource.BuiltIn(name)`, `AvatarSource.BuiltInNames`, an implicit conversion from a string and `AvatarSourceConverter`: a built-in name or an asset path in XAML.
- `VoiceAvatarDriver` moved to `Plugin.Maui.Spine.Controls.Avatar/Drivers` beside `SpeechAvatarDriver` (built for net10.0 and tested there); its VoiceView-only hooks (`Feed`, the host constructor) removed. `AvatarView.IsHosted`/`TickFromHost` and `SpineAvatarOptions.DefaultSource`, which only VoiceView used, removed.
- `Plugin.Maui.Spine.Controls.Voice` and its tests removed: `VoiceView`, `OrbVisual`, `AvatarVisual` (the canvas face), `VoiceVisual`, `VoiceVisualView`, `VoiceVisualRegistry`, `UseSpineVoiceView`, `VoiceAvatar`; out of the solution and the package filter.
- Tests: `BarsAndRingTests` (each bar and tick follows its own band, the app's colours replace the slots, Reduce Motion shows the level and not the spectrum; renders in `bin/…/sheets/` for review), `VoiceDriverTests.BarsAndRingGetTheSpectrumWhileTheConversationIsLive`.
- Showcase: Voice visuals removed (its reference speech is the Avatars detail page's Listen tab); Bars and Ring in the Avatars gallery from the built-in sources; Assistant without its AvatarView/VoiceView switch; Voice chat picks an avatar (`VoiceAvatars.All`: Bars, Ring, Nova, Puff, Voice Totem, Aurora Flow) instead of a VoiceView kind. Checked on the iOS simulator: bars and ring move with the reference speech and the microphone, and with the Text tab's estimated mouth; the microphone's side takes the app's accent.
- Docs: voice, avatars and packages wiki pages, the Controls.Avatar README, the root README, the spine-controls, spine-avatar and spine-setup skills, both plans (spine-avatar C2b and the package map; ai-and-security 4.3b marked superseded, a dated decision), the voice proposal's status.

## Decisions

- **One visual for a voice: AvatarView** (the owner, 2026-10-19): the orb is removed, not converted; the canvas face is retired (Nova and Puff do it better); the canvas plug-in contracts go (a look of one's own is a `.spineavatar`); the Showcase keeps one path.
- **No `Kind` property.** The owner suggested a kind setting; `Source` already picks the avatar, so a second property would be two ways to say the same thing. A built-in avatar is a source by name instead: `Source="bars"`.
- **The microphone in `accent`.** VoiceView's `InputColor` defaulted to the app's accent; the `accent` slot gives the same without a new property, and `AccentColor` overrides it. The answer is the `output` slot (`SetThemeColor`).
- **The driver in Controls.Avatar.** Spine.Voice is light (Common, Speech, the AI abstractions), and a separate package for one class would bring back the split the owner wanted gone.
- **Vector, not SkSL.** A shader is drawn on the CPU into a buffer of at most 112 px and scaled up (Android, Windows), which blurs bars; spine2d paths stay sharp everywhere.
- **One path node per band**, rotated to point up, down or outward and scaled along it from its inner end: a group plus a shape per band gave the ring 198 nodes, over the format's 128.
- **An empty first root node** in both packages: the scheduler's springs (squash, tilt, lift) move the first root, and a spectrum that wobbles reads as wrong.
- **Avatars keep their aspect** (`fit: contain`): in a very wide, short view the bars are smaller than a canvas that filled any rectangle.
- Posters are rendered under Reduce Motion by the tool, so bars' poster is its bell of bars.
