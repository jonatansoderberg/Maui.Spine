The Showcase shows what **Plugin.Maui.Spine** does in a real .NET MAUI app: navigation, sheets and the header bar, Liquid Glass, rows, lists, charts, widgets, notifications, sign-in, voice and avatars. Every page is one sample, with the code for it a tap away.

## Good to know

- The Showcase is a sample, not a product. Its data is made up, and pages may change or go away between builds.
- Sign-in, push notifications, chat, voice and generated images talk to the Spine sample server. A build for the simulator uses one on the developer's own machine.
- Some samples ask for the camera, the microphone, notifications or Face ID. They ask when you open them, and only use what they ask for on that page.

## Privacy

The Showcase collects nothing about you and has no analytics. What you type or say on a page that talks to the sample server is sent there to answer you, and is not kept for anything else.
